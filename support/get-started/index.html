Getting Started

Welcome to Icon Studio! This guide will help you understand the interface and build your very first customized icon from scratch.

Icon Studio provides a real-time canvas where you can customize layouts, symbols, gradients, and badges to design assets for websites, mobile applications, or anything you can think of.

Workspace Layout

The workspace is split into two primary areas:

  • The Preview Canvas: Displays your icon layout and customizations in real time.
  • The Tools Sidebar: Contains panels for configuring the base layer, style properties, gradients, borders, and badge overlays.

Creating Your First Icon

To begin design customization, navigate to the sidebar options:

  1. Choose a Base Layer: Choose between an Icon, custom Text, or your own uploaded Image.
  2. Adjust Canvas Styles: Select background properties like solid colors or multi-color gradients. You can also customize border radius, padding, and drop shadows.
  3. Configure Badges: Optionally add an icon inside a badge to the corner of your icon for a layered effect.

Exporting Designs

Once your icon is ready, you can can choose your export format to suit your needs. Icon Studio supports exporting high-resolution PNGs.

PNG Export

PNG is best for standard web and app assets where you need a fixed pixel dimension:

  • Click the Export PNG button in the primary actions section.
  • The PNG is rendered at high resolution to ensure crisp assets even on high-DPI (Retina) screens.
  • Transparency is preserved outside the custom icon container boundary.

Sharing & Collaboration

Icon Studio includes built-in sharing and collaboration features. To generate a unique link containing your current configuration:

  1. Click the Share Design button in the primary actions section.
  2. The current layout, color parameters, and configuration properties are compressed into query parameters and copied to your clipboard automatically.
  3. Send this link to anyone to let them view and edit the exact design.
